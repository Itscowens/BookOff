How it will work · planned features

Three steps to a simpler booking day

1

Set your availability

The business sets its opening hours, appointment slots and blocked times.

2

Customers choose a slot

Customers choose an available time slot and enter their booking details online.

3

See your bookings

The business sees bookings in one place, with occupied staff/time slots no longer offered.

These are planned Book Off features, not claims about the current daycare alpha. Vaccination details are not required for the proposed general booking system.
